## Core Identity
The main city of **Beyond the Veil** is a massive coastal metropolis, visually defined by towering skyscrapers, dense urban sprawl, neon-lit streets, and constant movement.

Its aesthetic feels like a fusion of **Tokyo and New York**, combining hyper-modern architecture, vertical city design, and overwhelming urban energy.

Despite its urban density, the city is surrounded and divided by extensive natural landscapes:
- massive public parks
- protected forest reserves
- rivers and canals crossing districts
- lakes and wetlands
- coastal cliffs and beaches
- nearby mountain ranges

This contrast between advanced civilization and overwhelming nature is one of the city's defining traits.

The city feels alive, bright, and futuristic, yet beneath its surface it hides a second ecosystem: the supernatural world.

---

## Why This City Matters
The city is a global academic and scientific hub, home to:
- one of the most prestigious universities in the world
- elite archaeology and anthropology departments
- cutting-edge research centers (physics, biology, materials science)
- museums and historical institutes
- a powerful medical research sector

This is the primary reason why the **Artifact Fragment** was transported here: officially for preservation and study.

Unofficially, multiple hidden factions ensured it would end up in this city.

---

## Geographic Layout
The city is built around three major geographic features:

### 1. The Bay / Coastline
A massive bay defines the eastern side of the city.
It contains:
- ports
- cargo terminals
- luxury marinas
- industrial shipyards
- small islands used for restricted operations

The coastline allows smuggling, secret rituals, and hidden entrances connected to Veil disturbances.

### 2. The River Network
Multiple rivers cross the city, dividing districts and feeding into lakes and canals.
The riverbanks contain parks, underground infrastructure, and forgotten tunnels.

Many supernatural incidents occur near water, as the Veil seems to resonate strongly with it.

### 3. The Northern Forest Reserve
A vast protected forest surrounds the northern edge of the city.
This is not a normal forest: it is unusually dense, ancient, and silent.

Local myths describe it as cursed.
The Council of Silence monitors this region closely, as it contains:
- frequent Veil anomalies
- unexplained disappearances
- ancient ruins hidden underground
- cult activity

---

## Political and Secret Influence
This city is important enough to host a **major regional headquarters** of the Council of Silence.

The Council operates under multiple covers:
- federal investigation divisions
- intelligence agencies
- private security contractors
- research institutions
- museum boards

The Royals maintain influence here through:
- corporate control
- university funding
- real estate monopolies
- private hospitals and laboratories

Because of the city's high supernatural concentration, it also attracts:
- Cults of the Veil
- Sintonizados seeking power
- Umbrin Purists hunting Veiled
- black market Veiled traffickers

---

## The City as a Supernatural Hotspot
The city has an abnormally high concentration of Veiled and supernatural activity due to:

- diverse ecosystems (urban + forests + rivers + ocean)
- constant human population pressure
- hidden underground infrastructure
- Veil energy "leaking" through unstable natural zones
- academic presence bringing artifacts and forbidden knowledge

The city is effectively a magnet for supernatural conflicts.

---

## Atmosphere and Tone
During the day, the city appears bright, modern, and alive.
At night, neon lights and rain reflect across streets, giving it an almost dreamlike look.

However, the city always carries a subtle feeling of unnatural tension:
- sudden silence in crowded places
- distorted reflections in glass buildings
- strange animal behavior near parks
- rumors of disappearances blamed on drugs or gangs

The supernatural world exists like a parasite beneath the surface.

---

## Main Story Relevance
The city is where the protagonist's journey evolves from isolated hunting into large-scale conflict.

It becomes the center point where all factions collide over the Artifact Fragment and the growing instability of the Veil.
