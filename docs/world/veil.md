# The Veil

## General Description
The Veil is a superior dimension resembling an infinite primordial forest, overflowing with supernatural energy.
It is a place of impossible beauty, often described as Eden-like, with lush vegetation and ancient lifeforms.

The Veil is not peaceful. Its beauty hides constant danger.

## Sky and Atmosphere
The Veil sky is not consistent with Earth's.
It contains multiple moons, unfamiliar constellations, and flowing streams of Veil energy visible as aurora-like trails.

The light is diffuse and unnatural, as if the forest itself emits illumination.

## Ruins of the Forgotten Civilization
Across the Veil exist massive ruins of an ancient civilization that once mastered Veil energy and created weapons capable of opposing entities beyond the Veil.

This civilization was erased from history, leaving behind:
- shattered temples
- impossible stone structures
- buried monoliths
- inscriptions in unknown languages
- broken fragments of technology-magic hybrids

The artifact found in the human world is believed to be a fragment of their weapon.

## Native Tribal Societies
After the fall of the ancient civilization, a new intelligent species evolved in the Veil.
They live in small tribal villages, forming survival-based societies.

They are not harmonious.
Their culture is shaped by constant fear of Veil Beasts, scarcity, and territorial survival.

## Remnants of the Ancient Civilization
Rare survivors or remnants may still exist:
- warped by Veil exposure
- bound to ruins or ancient sites
- intelligent but alien
- possessors of forbidden knowledge

They are considered myths even among Veil natives.

## Veil Beasts
Veil Beasts are violent and instinct-driven creatures born from Veil energy.
They are unpredictable and highly territorial.

Only extremely powerful Sintonizados can attempt to control them through spiritual suppression.

## The Umbrins and the Veil
Umbrins are NOT native to Veil tribal societies.
They are an anomaly manifested within humanity, described as the Veil's "antibody."

Their presence causes unnatural reactions in magic and rituals, often leading to catastrophic results.
