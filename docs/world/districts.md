# Districts of the City

## Overview
The city is divided into distinct districts, each with unique atmosphere, architecture, and supernatural presence.

Each district is designed to support investigation gameplay, faction conflicts, and varied combat encounters.

---

# 1. The Crown District (Financial & Political Core)

## Description
The Crown District is the city’s vertical heart: skyscrapers, corporate headquarters, luxury hotels, and elite government institutions.

Its streets are clean, heavily surveilled, and patrolled by private security forces.
Underground, however, the district contains sealed tunnels and restricted archives connected to the Council of Silence.

The Royals maintain significant influence here through corporations, political allies, and financial networks.

## Key Features
- high-rise skyline
- elite government buildings
- luxury apartments
- underground restricted zones
- corporate-controlled security

## Supernatural Presence
Veiled activity is rare in public, but extremely dense behind closed doors.
This is a district of hidden power, not open violence.

# 2. Neon Ward (Entertainment & Crime)

## Description
The Neon Ward is a district of constant artificial light.
Billboards, holograms, clubs, and underground venues create an overwhelming nightlife ecosystem.

It is filled with crime, illegal Veiled trafficking, cult recruitment, and black market rituals disguised as drug parties.

The Council monitors this area heavily, but control is fragile.

## Key Features
- neon streets and crowded alleys
- nightclubs and hidden cult basements
- black market dealers
- constant movement and noise
- illegal arenas and underground fights

## Supernatural Presence
Veiled transformations occur more often here because chaos hides mistakes.
Many disappearances are blamed on gangs or overdoses.

# 3. Harborline (Port & Industrial Zone)

## Description
Harborline is the city’s industrial backbone.
Cargo ships arrive daily, containers stack like walls, and shipyards operate nonstop.

Smuggling routes run through Harborline, making it a hotspot for:
- Veiled contraband
- ritual components
- captured creatures
- forbidden artifacts

Abandoned warehouses near the docks are often used by Cults of the Veil to open ruptures.

## Key Features
- shipping containers and cranes
- shipyards and industrial machinery
- abandoned warehouses
- foggy coastline
- hidden tunnels beneath docks

## Supernatural Presence
Veil energy disturbances often appear near the water.
Harborline is one of the most dangerous districts at night.

# 4. The Grand Campus (Academic & Research Zone)

## Description
The Grand Campus is a massive academic zone containing:
- universities
- research institutes
- museums
- libraries
- archaeological archives
- medical laboratories

It is heavily funded by corporate sponsors, many secretly controlled by Royals.

The Artifact Fragment was officially delivered here for analysis, making this district a high-priority target for every faction.

## Key Features
- modern university buildings
- museums and restricted vaults
- laboratories and research towers
- student population
- secret underground storage areas

## Supernatural Presence
Sintonizados often gather here, drawn by knowledge and proximity to artifacts.
The Council maintains constant surveillance.

# 5. Verdant Spine (Central Parks & Riverwalk)

## Description
Verdant Spine is a continuous chain of parks, botanical gardens, and riverwalk paths that stretch through the city.

This district is peaceful on the surface, filled with joggers and tourists.
But at night, it becomes a hunting ground.

Hidden within the trees are:
- forgotten shrines
- old ritual circles
- underground passages
- Veil-sensitive plants

## Key Features
- giant central park
- botanical gardens
- lakes and riverwalks
- stone bridges and tunnels
- abandoned shrines

## Supernatural Presence
Veiled sightings occur frequently.
The Veil seems unusually active here, as if the natural landscape amplifies its influence.

# 6. Ivory Heights (Upper-Class Residential)

## Description
Ivory Heights is a district of luxury homes, private mansions, and gated neighborhoods.

Many Royals hide here under false identities, living among political elites and billionaires.
The district is filled with private security and hidden underground estates.

The streets are quiet, polished, and unnaturally controlled.

## Key Features
- luxury mansions
- gated communities
- private gardens
- hidden underground halls
- elite clubs

## Supernatural Presence
Veiled activity is subtle but powerful.
This district feels calm, but predators live behind every gate.

# 7. The Undergrid (Subway & Forgotten Infrastructure)

## Description
The Undergrid is not officially a district, but an entire hidden layer beneath the city.

It includes:
- subway tunnels
- abandoned train stations
- maintenance corridors
- storm drains
- sealed research bunkers
- forgotten wartime shelters

This is where the city becomes claustrophobic and terrifying.

Many cult rituals happen here because the noise of the city above hides the screams.

## Key Features
- underground rail tunnels
- flickering lights
- flooded sections
- abandoned stations
- sealed doors with warning symbols

## Supernatural Presence
Ruptures often open in forgotten underground zones.
The Undergrid is a hunting territory for Veil Beasts and criminal Veiled.

# 8. The Northern Reserve (Forest & Mountains)

## Description
The Northern Reserve is a protected forest region bordering the city.

It contains:
- massive ancient trees
- hiking paths that lead into nowhere
- fog-covered valleys
- caves and mountain cliffs
- hidden ruins and Veil anomalies

This region is considered cursed by locals.
Disappearances are frequent, explained away as accidents.

The Council of Silence maintains secret patrols here, but control is unstable.

## Key Features
- dense forest
- cliffs and caves
- mountain trails
- hidden ruins
- frequent Veil distortions

## Supernatural Presence
This is one of the strongest Veil hotspots.
Cults of the Veil often attempt rituals here.

Umbrin Purists are frequently drawn to this region, believing it is a nest of corruption.

# 9. Greyfield Suburbs (Middle-Class Residential Zone)

## Description
Greyfield Suburbs is the city's quiet middle-class residential district: tree-lined streets, small family houses, local schools, grocery stores, and neighborhood parks.

During the day, it feels safe and ordinary.
At night, it becomes one of the most unsettling areas in the game, because it is where the supernatural blends seamlessly into everyday life.

This district is commonly linked to:
- unexplained disappearances
- attacks disguised as robberies or accidents
- cult infiltration inside local communities
- Resonants living in secrecy
- Veiled hiding as normal families for decades

Greyfield is the perfect setting for investigation quests with a psychological horror tone.

---

## Key Features
- suburban houses and quiet streets
- schools, churches, and local markets
- small parks and forest trails
- community surveillance and local patrols
- drainage tunnels leading into the Undergrid

---

## Supernatural Presence
Low.

Most Veiled living here remain in Human Form permanently, blending into society.
When supernatural activity occurs, it is usually tied to:
- kidnappings
- cult operations using basements
- rare Veil anomalies spreading from the Northern Reserve

# 10. Redline Quarter (Peripheral Zone / Slums)

## Description
Redline Quarter is the city's decaying peripheral district, formed by old housing projects, abandoned buildings, broken streets, and partially deactivated industrial zones.

It is defined by poverty, violence, and systemic neglect.
Regular police forces avoid entering certain areas, and even the Council of Silence struggles to maintain control.

This district is infamous for:
- drug trafficking and Veil-infused substances
- black market potion trade
- disappearances and kidnappings
- homeless people being used as living "batteries" for rituals
- underground arenas where Veiled fight for money
- illegal cult gatherings hidden inside ruins of buildings

The population often witnesses supernatural signs, but fear and manipulation keep them silent.

---

## Key Features
- abandoned apartment blocks
- narrow alleys and improvised tunnels
- illegal clinics and potion dens
- gangs mixed with cult activity
- unstable electricity and frequent fires
- sewer access leading into the Undergrid

---

## Supernatural Presence
Extremely high.

Veiled criminals often operate openly in **Veiled Form**, knowing that chaos hides their nature.
Cults of the Veil frequently perform rituals here, using victims as fuel.

Artificial Veil ruptures are far more common in Redline Quarter than in any other district.
