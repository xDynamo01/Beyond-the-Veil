# Beyond the Veil — Factions Overview

This document contains the official overview of all major factions operating in **Ravenport**, including their ideology, goals, methods, and role in the main story.

---

# 1. The Council of Silence

## Type
Secret Government Organization (Veiled + Human Allies)

## Overview
The Council of Silence is a covert organization embedded inside governments and intelligence agencies.
Its primary purpose is to maintain secrecy and prevent the supernatural world from being exposed to normal society.

The Council operates globally, but Ravenport hosts a major regional headquarters due to the city's unusually high concentration of Veil-related incidents.

## Core Goals
- Maintain the Veil secrecy at all costs
- Contain supernatural outbreaks and ruptures
- Silence witnesses, whistleblowers, and rogue agents
- Prevent public transformation events

## Methods
- surveillance networks and informants
- undercover agents
- disinformation campaigns
- controlled arrests and memory manipulation through intimidation
- confiscation of supernatural evidence

The Council does not always kill witnesses.
Most of the time, they suppress through pressure, control, or institutional imprisonment.

## Relationship With Other Factions
- Opposes Cults of the Veil
- Constant tension with Royals
- Uses Resonants as assets when necessary
- Knows the truth about Umbrin but treats them as unstable variables

# 2. The Royals (Royal Houses)

## Type
Veiled Aristocracy / Political Elite

## Overview
The Royals are pure-blood Veiled dynasties who once ruled humanity openly.
In modern times, they operate as a hidden aristocratic class, controlling society through wealth, politics, corporations, and influence.

Each Royal House originates from a distinct Veiled species.
Royal blood contains a stronger Veil saturation, granting them superior abilities.

However, Royals face a major weakness: using power too openly risks destabilizing reality.

## Core Rules
- Royals access full power only in Unveiled Form
- Prolonged power use increases Veil instability
- Royal power activates a detectable Royal Presence ("Royal Mark")
- This Presence attracts supernatural attention and increases rupture risk

## Core Goals
- Maintain dominance and secrecy
- Expand influence over Veil-related artifacts
- Control Resonants and Veiled society
- Prevent Umbrin interference
- Acquire the Artifact Fragment

## Methods
Royals avoid direct combat whenever possible.
They rely on:
- servants and loyal Veiled
- contracted Familiars
- bribed or controlled Resonants
- corporate and political systems

## Relationship With Other Factions
- Manipulates the Council of Silence but fears exposure
- Opposes Veiled Restorationists when their chaos threatens stability
- Uses Cults of the Veil indirectly when useful
- Treats Umbrin as an existential threat

# 3. Veiled Restorationists

## Type
Veiled Supremacist Movement

## Overview
The Veiled Restorationists are extremist Veiled supremacists who believe the old era must return: a time when Veiled ruled humanity openly and humans existed as inferior beings.

They reject secrecy and view the Council of Silence as weak and cowardly.

## Core Goals
- Restore Veiled dominance
- Break the secrecy system
- Establish new hierarchy over humans
- Acquire the Artifact Fragment to increase Veil power

## Methods
- violent intimidation campaigns
- assassinations of Council agents
- forced Veiled recruitment
- public terror events disguised as criminal violence
- ritual support from cult networks

## Relationship With Other Factions
- Hated by Umbrin Purists
- Dangerous rivals to Royals
- Constant enemies of the Council
- Uses Cults of the Veil when convenient

# 4. Umbrin Purists

## Type
Extremist Hunter Faction

## Overview
The Umbrin Purists are a radical ideology that believes all Veiled must be eliminated, regardless of guilt, innocence, or integration.

They view the Veiled as a cancer infecting humanity.

Unlike independent Umbrin hunters, Purists are organized, ruthless, and ideological.

## Core Goals
- Eradicate all Veiled
- Destroy Veil-related artifacts
- Execute Resonants and cultists as corruption carriers
- Hunt Royals as priority targets

## Methods
- brutal ambush tactics
- public executions disguised as disappearances
- infiltration of Veiled communities
- weaponization of cursed objects and Veil-resistant materials

## Relationship With Other Factions
- Direct enemy of all Veiled
- Hated by the Council of Silence (because they cause exposure)
- Seen as unstable threats by Royals
- Potential temporary enemies of Cults, but ideological conflict remains

## Symbol Prompt

# 5. Cults of the Veil

## Type
Ritualistic Fanatics

## Overview
The Cults of the Veil are fragmented groups of fanatics who worship the Veil as divine truth.
They believe ruptures are not disasters, but sacred openings.

They perform rituals to gain power, summon beings, or create artificial Veil breaches.

## Core Beliefs
- The Veil is sacred and should consume reality
- Blood and sacrifice are valid currencies
- Veil Beasts are divine messengers

## Core Goals
- Open ruptures and spread Veil influence
- Summon or bind Veil entities
- Acquire the Artifact Fragment
- Sacrifice Veiled and Royals to amplify rituals

## Methods
- sacrifice-based rituals
- kidnapping operations (especially in Redline Quarter)
- secret underground gatherings
- infiltration of drug markets and black-market potion trade

## Relationship With Other Factions
- Opposed by the Council
- Sometimes funded indirectly by Royals
- Uses Resonants as ritual specialists
- Hunted by Umbrin Purists

# 6. Resonants (Sintonizados)

## Type
Human Veil Channelers

## Overview
Resonants are humans capable of perceiving and interacting with Veil energy.
They can see Veiled in Veiled Form and Unveiled Form.

Most Resonants are physically marked by Veil exposure through deformities known as Veil Scars.

The strongest Resonants often show no visible scars.

## Core Traits
- ability to sense Veil distortions
- limited spellcraft and potion-making
- high risk of corruption or collapse

## Microfissures
Advanced Resonants can open microfissures into the Veil to temporarily amplify their abilities.
This power can rival Royal blood, but causes severe consequences:
- collapse and organ failure
- mental instability
- risk of death
- possible rupture escalation

## Veil Beast Suppression
Only powerful Resonants can attempt to control Veil Beasts through spiritual union.
Success grants access to Beast abilities.
Failure can cause insanity, possession, or catastrophic rupture events.

## Relationship With Other Factions
- hunted by Umbrin Purists
- manipulated or enslaved by Royals
- recruited by Cults of the Veil
- monitored by the Council of Silence

# 7. Veil Familiars

## Type
Contracted Entities

## Overview
Familiars are Veil entities summoned through rituals and bound through contracts.
They are not mindless creatures. They have motives, personalities, and unpredictable behavior.

Some Familiars are guardians.
Others are parasites, opportunists, or manipulators.

## Contracts
Contracts vary in complexity:
- trivial demands (food, pleasure, sensations)
- long-term loyalty pacts
- blood payment agreements
- service in exchange for protection

Familiars often exploit loopholes in contracts.

## Relationship With Other Factions
- used heavily by Royals as indirect weapons
- summoned by Cults of the Veil
- feared by the Council
- hunted by Umbrin Purists when discovered

# 8. The Conspiracy Network (Human Faction)

## Working Name
The Black Signal

## Type
Human Conspiracy Movement / Underground Network

## Overview
The Black Signal is a decentralized human conspiracy faction operating through encrypted forums, pirate radio broadcasts, underground meetups, and hidden document archives.

They believe Ravenport is a containment zone where governments hide non-human entities and ritual crimes.

They are not entirely wrong.

Some members are harmless paranoids.
Others are violent extremists dangerously close to discovering the truth.

## Core Goals
Publicly:
- expose the supernatural world

Privately:
- weaponize the truth
- sell information to criminals or corporations
- capture Veiled as proof

## Methods
- surveillance teams and stalking operations
- hacking, data mining, and archive theft
- infiltration of hospitals, police departments, and universities
- kidnapping suspected Veiled or Resonants
- amateur rituals based on stolen knowledge

## Structure
- Researchers (archivists, hackers, theorists)
- Watchers (field surveillance teams)
- Armed Cells (militia-style extremists)

## Narrative Role
The Black Signal is a destabilizing force.
They do not have supernatural power, but they threaten the secrecy system.

Their actions can cause:
- exposure crises
- accidental rupture events
- mass paranoia
- public panic if proof spreads

## Relationship With Other Factions
- hunted by the Council of Silence
- exploited by Cults of the Veil
- targeted by Royals if they get too close
- may cooperate with Umbrin Purists without understanding the danger
