## Overview
Umbrins are extremely rare anomalies within humanity.
Among Velados, they are treated as legends and horror stories.

Many Velados believe Umbrins are not real.

## Perception Effect
When a Velado sees an Umbrin, they experience:
- unnatural black eyes
- dark markings around the eyes
- overwhelming fear and instinctive terror

This reaction feels like the Velado is staring at its own predatory nature.

Sunglasses can partially hide this perception.

## Nature
Umbrins are not simply humans with training.
They are closer to a separate species, with:
- heightened strength and speed
- instinctive combat awareness
- resistance to supernatural influence

Magic and cursed weapons often react unpredictably against Umbrins.

## Ancient Name
The forgotten civilization recorded Umbrins under the name:
**Profanus**
A term of contempt, meaning beings rejected by the sacred nature of the Veil.

Only later did the ancient civilization realize Umbrins were the perfect weapon against Veil invasions.

## Umbrin Royals
Through generations of strategic breeding, Royals attempted to control Umbrins.
This created Umbrin Royals: individuals with consistent inheritance of Umbrin traits and Royal blood.

Unlike other Royals, Umbrin Royals do not create ruptures through direct power use.
They are seen as the Veil's "antibody," capable of channeling power without destabilizing reality.
