## Overview
Veiled are supernatural beings living hidden among human society.
They exist in multiple species, each with different traits and forms.

Most Veiled conceal their nature to survive and to avoid attracting attention from:
- the Council of Silence
- Umbrin Purists
- Cults of the Veil
- rival factions

## The Three Forms
Veiled possess three distinct states of manifestation:

### 1. Human Form
A complete disguise.
No visible supernatural anatomy exists.
Most abilities are suppressed.

### 2. Veiled Form
Partial manifestation.
Only Veiled, Umbrins, and rare humans (Sintonizados) can perceive this form.
It grants access to enhanced senses and partial supernatural strength.

### 3. Unveiled Form
The true form.
Visible to everyone.
This form grants maximum physical strength and access to full abilities.

This form is dangerous to use publicly because it breaks the illusion of secrecy.

## Power Rules
- Most Veiled have similar baseline "Veil density" in their blood.
- Royals are naturally above this baseline.
- Veil exposure and age can increase saturation over time.

## Cultural Notes
Veiled often refer to normal humans using an old term:
**"Leer"**, meaning "the empty ones."
This reflects their belief that humans are unaware and spiritually uncharged.
