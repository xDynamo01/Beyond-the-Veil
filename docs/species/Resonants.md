## Overview
Resonants are humans capable of perceiving the supernatural world.
They can see Veiled in both **Veiled Form** and **Unveiled Form**, and often sense disturbances connected to the Veil.

To most normal humans (Leer), the supernatural remains invisible.
To Resonants, it feels like a second reality layered on top of the first.

Veiled can often detect Resonants by instinct, describing them as humans with an unnatural presence or "wrong aura."



---

## Veil Scars
Most Resonants develop deformities known as **Veil Scars**.

These scars can appear as:
- unusual veins or discoloration
- eye abnormalities
- burn-like patterns
- ritual-shaped marks
- subtle bone distortion

However, the most powerful Resonants often show no visible scars, making them harder to identify.

## Natural Abilities
Even without rituals, Resonants can display abilities such as:
- sensing supernatural presence
- perceiving Veiled transformations
- resistance to certain illusions or mental influence
- heightened intuition near Veil activity

Their perception is not simply "seeing monsters."
They experience the world differently, often feeling unstable or distorted when near Veil energy.

---

## Microfissures (Controlled Veil Access)
Advanced Resonants can open temporary **Microfissures** into the Veil to gain a short-lived power boost.

This boost can briefly rival the strength of Royal blood.

### Consequences
Opening Microfissures comes with severe cost:
- extreme fatigue
- internal bleeding or organ damage
- risk of death
- psychological instability
- possible loss of control leading to a full Veil rupture

The stronger the Resonant, the more precise their control.
But no Resonant is fully safe from the Veil’s backlash.

---

## Veil Beast Suppression (Spiritual Union)
Only extremely powerful Resonants can attempt to control Veil Beasts.

This is not traditional domination.
It requires a dangerous spiritual process often described as a forced **union**.

The Resonant must suppress the Beast’s will and bind it into submission.
If successful, the Resonant can channel part of the Beast’s abilities.

### Failure Outcomes
Failure is catastrophic and may result in:
- insanity and loss of identity
- possession by the Beast
- permanent mutation
- rupture-level incidents

For this reason, most Resonants refuse to attempt Beast suppression.

---
## Prime Resonants
Prime Resonants are extremely rare individuals who reached an abnormal level of synchronization with the Veil.

Unlike common Resonants, Prime Resonants often display:
- minimal or no visible Veil Scars
- stronger perception of Veil distortions
- the ability to open Microfissures with greater stability
- partial resistance to Veil backlash

Some Prime Resonants are capable of suppressing Veil Beasts more reliably, making them highly valuable assets.

### Threat Level
Prime Resonants are considered high-risk individuals.
The Council of Silence monitors them closely, and Royals actively seek to recruit or control them.

## Relationship with Royals
Royals rarely fight directly due to the risk of exposure, Veil rupture, and activation of their Royal Presence.

Instead, many Royals:
- hire Resonants as covert weapons
- blackmail or manipulate them
- fund illegal research on Microfissures
- attempt to weaponize Beast suppression

Some Resonants willingly serve Royals in exchange for resources, protection, and forbidden knowledge.

---

## Resonants and Umbrins
When a Resonant looks at an Umbrin, the effect is not simply fear.

They perceive something closer to raw Veil chaos:
a contradiction that feels unnatural, violent, and impossible.

Many Resonants describe it as:
- staring into an abyss
- hearing silence screaming
- seeing the Veil reject itself

This reaction often causes panic, nausea, or mental collapse.
